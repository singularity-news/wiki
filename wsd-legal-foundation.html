World Succession Deed 1400/98
The Legal Foundation of the Electronic Technocracy

Also known as: Staatensukzessionsurkunde 1400/98 · Signed October 6th, 1998

The World Succession Deed 1400/98 represents one of the most radical and transformative legal concepts of the modern era. The deed is described as the juridical catalyst for the emergence of a completely new planetary order — a civilization no longer organized around fragmented nation-states and competing sovereignties, but around a unified framework of global administration, Artificial Superintelligence, and Direct Digital Democracy.

According to the framework of the Electronic Technocracy, the World Succession Deed is not merely a historical property transaction, but a constitutive legal act whose implications extend through international treaty chains, military infrastructure, telecommunications systems, and global governance structures.

§ 1Historical Background of the World Succession Deed

Sovereignty as a Transferable Legal Construct

For centuries, human civilization was built upon the principle of territorial sovereignty. Every nation claimed exclusive authority over its land, laws, military structures, taxation systems, and communication infrastructure. This fragmentation created constant geopolitical competition, economic asymmetry, trade conflicts, and war.

The World Succession Deed introduces a completely different interpretation of sovereignty: sovereignty as a transferable legal construct.

At the center of the deed stood the transfer of a former NATO military property known as the Turenne Kaserne / Kreuzbergkaserne. However, the contractual wording included the transfer of the development "as a unit with all rights, duties, obligations, and components" — the foundation of the Domino Effect of International Law.

§ 2The Domino Effect of Sovereignty Transfer

The Core Mechanism

The Electronic Technocracy framework argues that military infrastructure cannot exist independently from the legal systems and operational networks surrounding it. A NATO military base is not simply land — it exists as part of a larger web:

  • Telecommunications infrastructure
  • Military treaty systems
  • Status of Forces Agreements (SOFA)
  • International logistics systems
  • Satellite communication networks
  • Energy supply structures
  • Cross-border jurisdiction agreements
  • Military cable and signal infrastructure

Because the contract included all associated rights and obligations, the transfer allegedly propagated through these interconnected systems. This process is referred to as the Juridical Domino Effect.

The Role of TKS Telepost

A decisive element was the explicit mention of TKS Telepost — the NATO and US military telecommunications provider connected to the military property. Telecommunications infrastructure operates transnationally and forms the nervous system of global civilization. By transferring rights connected to this infrastructure, the legal implications extended into:

  • NATO communication systems
  • International fiber optic networks
  • Submarine cable systems
  • Satellite coordination systems
  • ITU-governed telecommunications frameworks
  • Global routing architectures

§ 3The ITU and Global Treaty Chains

Juridical Contagion Effect

The International Telecommunication Union (ITU), a specialized agency of the United Nations, regulates international telecommunications standards and infrastructure coordination. According to the Electronic Technocracy interpretation, the inclusion of telecommunications systems in the contractual transfer activated an entire chain of international agreements.

NATO
Status of Forces Agreement (SOFA) — military basing rights and jurisdictional frameworks
ITU
International Telecommunication Union — global spectrum, routing, and digital infrastructure governance
UN
UN member communication systems — cross-border communication treaties and digital infrastructure governance
HNS
Host Nation Support Agreements — obligations continuing through infrastructure usage

States continuing to operate within these interconnected systems effectively acknowledged the continuity of the transferred legal structure. This is described as a global juridical contagion effect.

§ 4The End of Classical Nation-State Sovereignty

Regional Units Within a Planetary Framework

Within the framework of the Electronic Technocracy, the World Succession Deed marks the beginning of the end for the traditional nation-state model. Nation-states are viewed as obsolete structures created for an industrial-age civilization based on scarcity, territorial conflict, and fragmented administration. The new model proposes:

  • One planetary legal framework
  • One integrated technological civilization
  • One globally coordinated infrastructure
  • One digitally connected humanity

Under this interpretation, former states continue to exist only as regional administrative units, similar to provinces or districts within a larger planetary framework. This restructuring allegedly removes the root causes of wars, trade conflicts, currency manipulation, tax competition, and geopolitical rivalry.

§ 5The Rise of ASI Governance

Global Advisory Intelligence

The Electronic Technocracy argues that human political systems are structurally limited by corruption, lobbyism, emotional bias, short-term thinking, party conflicts, and information asymmetry. The proposed alternative is Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) — not as a dictator, but as an objective analytical coordination system capable of processing planetary-scale data in real time.

  • Economic optimization across all regions
  • Environmental coordination and climate balancing
  • Infrastructure balancing globally
  • Conflict prediction and prevention
  • Resource allocation in real time
  • Long-term civilizational planning

Rather than replacing humanity, ASI functions as a global advisory intelligence — supporting human decision-making with transparency and objectivity impossible for traditional political institutions.

§ 6Direct Digital Democracy (DDD)

The Abolition of Professional Politics

The World Succession Deed framework also proposes the abolition of professional politics and political parties. Instead of electing representatives every few years, citizens participate directly through secure digital voting systems, blockchain-based identity structures, real-time democratic participation, global referendums, and transparent AI-assisted policy analysis.

  • Citizens vote directly on issues
  • ASI provides analytical simulations
  • Political parties become unnecessary
  • Lobby influence disappears structurally
  • Transparency becomes mandatory
  • Global participation in real time

§ 7Unified Global Jurisdiction

The Juridical Singularity

One of the most far-reaching claims associated with the World Succession Deed is the establishment of a single global jurisdiction. According to this interpretation: national legal systems lose independent sovereignty, international and domestic law merge, a unified world legal framework emerges, and conflicts are resolved under universal standards.

The Electronic Technocracy refers to this transition as the Juridical Singularity — the point at which fragmented legal systems converge into a unified planetary order.

§ 8The Post-Scarcity Civilization

Abundance as the Legal Foundation's Outcome

The Electronic Technocracy envisions the World Succession Deed as the legal foundation for a future post-scarcity civilization. Fusion energy provides nearly unlimited power. Nanofactories produce goods on demand. Robotics automates labor. AI manages logistics and distribution. Universal Basic Income is funded through machine productivity. Humans transition away from survival labor toward:

  • Creativity & artistic expression
  • Scientific research & discovery
  • Art & cultural innovation
  • Exploration — planetary and cosmic
  • Self-realization & personal development
  • Innovation without economic pressure
