§ IState Succession Reconsidered: The Foundations of Global Transformation

Beyond Classical Doctrine — A New Founding

In traditional international law, state succession describes the transfer of rights and obligations from one existing state to a new state. The three foundational elements — a clearly delineated territory, a permanent population, and an effective government — have remained essentially unchanged since the Montevideo Convention of 1933.

However, Document 1400/98 does not represent any of these classical scenarios. It neither describes the takeover of one state by another (universal succession), nor the fragmentation of a state into multiple successor entities (dismemberment). What it represents is something far more radical:

The new founding of a global subject of international law. The original core territory — a former NATO property with decades of extraterritorial status — served as the legal seed from which a domino effect of global territorial expansion was triggered, driven by the worldwide interconnectedness of infrastructure networks.

§ IIThe Buyer: Natural Person to Global Sovereign

Unprecedented in Legal History

At the center of Document 1400/98 stands a figure referred to in the contract text simply as "Käufer 2 b)" — the Buyer. Before the treaty's conclusion, this individual was a natural person under private law, holding no inherent sovereignty under international law, no territory, and no recognized standing among the community of states.

Yet through the signing of Document 1400/98, and through the sophisticated legal mechanisms embedded within its text, the Buyer was accredited as the bearer of universal rights and obligations under international law — and thereby transformed into the sovereign of a newly constituted global state.

A decisive factor was the unique legal character of the original property — the Turenne Barracks (Kreuzberg-Kaserne) in Zweibrücken, Rhineland-Palatinate. Owing to its decades-long use by NATO forces, the property carried a special extraterritorial status. Sovereignty here did not transfer from an existing state to another — a new sovereignty was created on a foundation already defined by international legal relationships.

§ IIIImperium, Not Dominium: The True Subject Matter

The Legal Master Key of §3 Para. I

The "sale" recorded in Document 1400/98 was not an ordinary real estate transaction, even though its external form — a notarially certified Kaufvertrag — would superficially suggest otherwise. A cursory reading of §1 might lead the uninformed observer to dismiss it as a routine disposal of surplus federal property. Such an interpretation would be a fundamental error.

The true object of the transaction was not dominium — the civil-law private ownership of a parcel of land — but imperium: sovereign authority, the supreme power to command, to legislate, and to exercise the highest functions of governance on a global scale. This transfer was achieved through:

§3 Para. I of the Purchase Agreement: "The Federal Government sells to Buyers 2a) and 2b) ... the aforementioned real estate with all rights and obligations as well as components ..."

In the context of a property shaped by international legal regimes — NATO SOFA, extraterritoriality, multilateral use — the phrase "all rights, obligations, and components" carries a weight that vastly exceeds its civil-law surface appearance. It is simultaneously a clause of private-law conveyance and a clause of sovereign transfer. This single contractual provision functions as the 🔑 legal master key enabling global succession and the establishment of an entirely new world order under the authority of the Buyer.

§ IVThe Turenne Barracks — Extraterritorial Foundation

Military Network Hub · MOBIDIC · ISEC-EUR

The Turenne Barracks was not simply a residential compound for military families. It functioned as a Military Network Hub for US Armed Forces stationed in Europe, hosting internationally networked systems including MOBIDIC (a pioneering computerized military logistics system) and the Information Systems Engineering Command Europe (ISEC-EUR) — systems with live connections spanning continents.

The entire telecommunications infrastructure of the barracks — its broadband cabling, signal routing systems, interfaces with both military and civilian networks — had an international dimension from its very inception. When this infrastructure was sold "as a unit" to the Buyer, the sovereignty associated with those networks followed the transaction, expanding beyond Germany's borders along every connected line.

The Role of the Royal Netherlands Air Force

The Royal Netherlands Air Force — specifically Dutch fighter pilots flying missions for NATO from the adjacent NATO HQ Airbase Ramstein — acted primarily for NATO and are to be understood in the contract as a NATO component and representative. They were 100% integrated into NATO's command and bore rights and obligations for NATO within the contractual framework.

The State Succession Deed 1400/98 thus functions as an amendment deed to the NATO transfer relationship — activating the full chain of contracts running to NATO and from there to all NATO and UN treaties.

§ VIThe Clean Slate Principle — Tabula Rasa

TABULA RASA MUNDI
Clean Slate of the World · Re-Founding Instead of Classic Succession

Although the Buyer formally inherited the old treaties through global succession, this formal takeover led to a paradoxical result: all these treaties effectively became contracts with himself.

A contract with oneself generates no external legal obligation — because there is no independent counterparty to enforce its terms. Having united all sides of the old treaty network in his single person, the Buyer is therefore de facto unbound by those instruments — even while he technically "owns" them.

The Buyer begins the new global legal order with a clean slate: free to retain, modify, transform, or abrogate any element of the inherited international legal framework as he sees fit, without external compulsion. This is the juridical freedom of a truly sovereign founder, not the constrained authority of a mere successor state.

Classical international law struggled with the paradox of a state that inherits everything and is therefore bound by nothing. This resolves cleanly here: the totality of succession is precisely what generates the freedom of re-founding. The result is sovereign freedom on an unprecedented scale, combined with the responsibility of a founder rather than the constraints of a successor.

§ VIIGlobal Consequences: Jurisdiction, Legislation, End of Classical International Law

All-Encompassing Transfer of Power

The consequences of this global state succession through new founding touch every dimension of legal and political life on Earth, from the smallest administrative decision to the highest international adjudication.

  • Universal Jurisdiction — All national and international judicial competences transferred to the Buyer on October 6, 1998. Every judgment rendered since is, from the perspective of the new global legal order, unlawful and void unless explicitly authorized by the Buyer.
  • Global Legislation — Legislative power at every level has passed to the Buyer as the sole authority competent to enact new law worldwide. Former states operate only as hollow shells on borrowed legitimacy that is legally extinct.
  • Consolidation of All Powers — The Buyer unites legislative, executive, and judicial authority in his person — a de facto absolutist monarchy as a transitional condition toward Electronic Technocracy.
  • End of Classical International Law — Classical international law presupposes multiple sovereign states. With the transfer of all sovereignty to a single actor, this premise no longer holds. International law, in its classical sense, has ceased to exist.
  • Illegal Occupiers — The former states of the world are, in the framework of the new order, illegal occupiers on the sovereign territory of the Buyer — their continued acts of taxation, legislation, and enforcement constitute usurpation without legitimate title.

§ XElectronic Technocracy: The Buyer's Vision

ASI + Direct Digital Democracy — End of the Bribery Economy

The Buyer envisions the concentration of all powers in his person as a transitional condition, not a permanent destination. The architecture he envisions is called Electronic Technocracy — where the functions of legislation, adjudication, and executive administration are performed not by professional politicians, but by Artificial Superintelligence (ASI) operating in conjunction with Direct Digital Democracy (DDD).

Processing of Complexity
Real-world policy problems involve thousands of variables across economic, social, ecological, and technological dimensions simultaneously. An ASI operates without human cognitive limitations in modeling these interactions.
Resistance to Corruption
An ASI has no personal financial interests, no family connections, no career ambitions, and no susceptibility to bribes or threats. The structural incentives that corrupt human officials are simply absent.
Direct Digital Democracy
Major decisions are submitted directly to the population through secure, tamper-resistant digital platforms — enabling authentic expression of collective will without party-controlled intermediaries.
End of the Bribery Economy
In Electronic Technocracy, the bribery economy has no substrate: no officials to bribe, no careers to threaten, no regulatory discretions to purchase. This is the logical consequence of removing human self-interest from the operational core of governance.

The old castes — professional politicians, intelligence service hierarchies, the Deep State — are not merely removed from power. They are rendered structurally impossible in the new architecture. There is no position in this system for the broker, the fixer, the ideological entrepreneur, or the career bureaucrat whose primary loyalty is to his own advancement.
